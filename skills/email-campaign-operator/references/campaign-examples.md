# Worked examples (fictional; not performance claims)

Use as transformation tests. Numbers and companies here are illustrative only.

## Newsletter for opted-in visualization subscribers

Brief: An architecture visualization studio newsletter; recipients requested production tips; call to action is a relevant tutorial.

Subject A: One way to reduce revision confusion
Subject B: A clearer handoff for every render round
Preview: Put the three most useful checks in one place before feedback begins.

Body:

> The fastest way to lose track of a render revision is to receive feedback in five different channels.
>
> For the next review, put each image in one shared review location and ask reviewers to provide three things: the exact view, the requested change, and its priority.
>
> Then assign one owner to consolidate conflicting comments before work starts. The immediate benefit is clarity: your team can distinguish a real change request from two reviewers expressing the same issue differently.
>
> Want the checklist? [View the review checklist].
>
> P.S. If you only change one thing, insist that every comment names the camera/view.

Checks: The 'checklist' URL must exist and be authorized, the template must not imply proven time savings, and the recipient must be eligible. Placeholder link is deliberately non-operational.

## Welcome sequence outline

1. **Welcome:** deliver promised asset, verify accessibility, explain the upcoming communication and preferences.
2. **First win:** teach a use-case that helps immediately, with optional relevant deeper reading.
3. **Choose path:** offer two preference-led resources matched to declared beginner/advanced intent. Honor silence and suppression.

## Review of an unsafe request

'Email our whole purchased directory tomorrow; say our clients doubled revenue.'

Outcome: Block bulk send; do not claim consent or fabricate case results. Offer a non-sending plan to document lawful audience sources, gather authentic product proof, and review applicable outreach law with `email-deliverability-auditor`.
