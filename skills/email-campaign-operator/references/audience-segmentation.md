# Audience segmentation and offer fit

Use this when defining who receives which message. Segment on legitimate, reliable signals, not speculative personal traits.

## Inputs and decision order

1. **Eligibility first:** contact origin, consent/other applicable lawful basis, product/marketing preference, opt-out state, suppression state, geography. No copy quality can repair an ineligible audience.
2. **Lifecycle:** new subscriber, engaged learner, qualified evaluator, active customer, lapsed subscriber.
3. **Declared needs:** use case, role, skill level, product category interest, explicitly stated preference. Prefer subscriber-provided data over inferred labels.
4. **Observed engagement:** recent relevant clicks, purchases, replies, or requested resources. Treat pixel opens cautiously; they are unreliable for individual-level engagement.
5. **Contact pressure:** messages already received, fatigue signals, complaint/unsubscribe trend, overlapping automations.

## Relevance matrix

| Segment | Promise | Useful content | Typical next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New opt-in | Fulfill what they requested and set expectations | Requested resource plus one immediate tactic | Access resource / choose interests |
| Beginner / exploration | Make first progress practical | Plain-language starter action | Read guide or see an example |
| Skilled / evaluator | Show a specific advanced method | Technical breakdown or credible comparison | Evaluate detailed case or demo |
| Current customer | Help them get value from what they already use | Adoption tip, known release, relevant workflow | Try the feature or request help |
| Quiet subscriber | Respect changing intent | One carefully scoped, high-value relevance check | Confirm preferences or leave |

## Guardrails

- Do not assign a higher-revenue or sophistication label when source evidence is missing.
- Do not collect sensitive attributes just to personalize a marketing message.
- A segment must meaningfully change content, offer, CTA, timing, or frequency; otherwise avoid gratuitous complexity.
- If the same campaign is sent to multiple segments, articulate the one shared need and test whether it genuinely applies.
- Treat a resubscription invitation differently from a revoked marketing permission: suppressed/opted-out contacts must not be re-marketed to unless a valid new permission is independently established.
