# Email-marketing policy and legal review

Last reviewed: 2026-10-19. Not legal advice. Determine where senders and recipients are located and who operates the campaign; do not assume one legal rule applies globally. Escalate uncertain high-volume or cross-border outreach for qualified counsel review.

## Three distinct sources of obligations

1. **Mail-provider acceptance policies:** Google and Yahoo authentication, complaints, and unsubscribes. These are service conditions, not laws.
2. **Marketing/anti-spam laws:** recipient country or regional rules on unsolicited marketing, identification and opt-out.
3. **Personal-data/privacy law:** lawful processing, transparency, security, minimization, retention, processor contracts, and data-subject rights.

## United States (CAN-SPAM)

Primary source: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business

For commercial email, do not falsify From or subject; provide applicable advertisement identification, valid physical postal address, clear opt-out, and honor it within the law's deadline (the FTC guide describes 10 business days). The opt-out mechanism must work for at least 30 days after sending. CAN-SPAM applies to B2B commercial email as well; prior express opt-in is not a universal statutory prerequisite under this specific law, although provider policy and other law may be stricter. Do not treat legality under CAN-SPAM as permission to use scraped lists.

## Indonesia (UU 27/2022)

Primary text/index: https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/229798/uu-no-27-

Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law regulates processing of personal data, controller/processor obligations, and rights of data subjects. Determine the specific applicable lawful basis and notice; don't assert that consent is the *only* lawful basis. Check additional electronic-communication, consumer-protection, and implementing rules as relevant. Verify current amendments, effective enforcement, and sector rules with official legal sources before advising a live campaign.

## EU/EEA and UK

EU GDPR: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj
EU ePrivacy Directive: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2002/58/oj

UK ICO direct marketing guidance: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/

GDPR lawful processing and electronic-direct-marketing permission are distinct issues. Local ePrivacy/PECR rules, B2B/B2C distinctions, soft opt-in conditions, and national implementations may materially differ. Do not give a universal 'legitimate interest allows cold email' or 'all B2B requires consent' shortcut.

## Pre-send decision record

`Recipient regions | Message purpose | Contact source and basis | Applicable provider rules | Applicable legal rules | Proven unsubscribe/suppression | Evidence gaps | Approver`.

If evidence is missing, the decision should be `draft only / send blocked pending review`, not a speculative legal clearance.
